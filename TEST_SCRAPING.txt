   🧪 HOW TO TEST LINKEDIN SCRAPING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: LOGIN TO YOUR DASHBOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   1. Open: http://localhost:3000/login
   2. Login with your account (or register if needed)
   3. This saves your auth token to localStorage


STEP 2: GO TO LINKEDIN MY JOBS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   1. Open: https://www.linkedin.com/my-items/applied-jobs/
   2. Make sure you're logged into LinkedIn
   3. Click the "Applied" tab (important!)
   4. Wait for jobs to load (scroll down if needed)


STEP 3: OPEN BROWSER CONSOLE (F12)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   This will show you detailed scraping logs:
   - 🎯 Starting capture
   - 📋 Found X job cards
   - ✓ Extracted: [Job] at [Company]
   - ✅ Captured X jobs


STEP 4: CLICK EXTENSION & CAPTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   1. Click the "UK Jobs Insider" extension icon (top right)
   2. Click "📥 Capture My Applied Jobs"
   3. Watch console for logs
   4. Wait for "✅ Successfully imported X jobs!"


STEP 5: CHECK YOUR DASHBOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   1. Go back to: http://localhost:3000/dashboard
   2. Refresh the page
   3. Your scraped jobs should appear!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: "Not on LinkedIn Applied Jobs page"
→ Make sure URL contains: /my-items/applied-jobs/

Problem: "No job cards found"
→ Make sure you clicked "Applied" tab, not "Saved"
→ Scroll down to load more jobs
→ Wait for page to fully load

Problem: "No auth token found"
→ Login at http://localhost:3000/login
→ Open extension popup
→ Click "🔄 Sync Token" button

Problem: Backend not responding
→ Check: docker-compose ps
→ Check logs: docker-compose logs backend
→ Restart: docker-compose restart backend
